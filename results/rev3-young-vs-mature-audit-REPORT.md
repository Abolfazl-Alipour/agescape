- **Central question**: Which results in the frozen PLOS ONE manuscript compare young animals or young adults against older adults (capturing developmental, post-juvenile, or maturational transitions), versus which isolate aging strictly from adulthood or maturity onward (`>= 18 months` in mouse, `>= 30 years` in human brain/lung mature refits, `>= 30 days` in fly, `>= day 6` in worm)?

## 1. Executive Audit Summary

Across all major analytical families in [final-plos-one.tex](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/report/plos-one/final-plos-one.tex), aging effects are **strongly concentrated in comparisons that include young animals or young-to-mature transitions**, whereas **mature-to-old signals are weaker, fewer, or methodologically underpowered** in the non-human datasets, while human adult (`>= 20 years`) and mature (`>= 30 years`) analyses preserve only specific compositional and interneuron transcriptional shifts:

1. **Mouse derived aging signature (`P1_Clean`, 139 genes)**: **100% a two-group `3 months vs 18 months` contrast** ([m2-unit-eligibility.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/m2-unit-eligibility.csv)), not an old-age (`18m vs 24m/30m`) contrast. In downstream human scoring (`252` tests across `11` cohorts spanning `2 to 89 years` in Table 1 & Table 2, `3` global BH hits), none beat matched random signatures (`lowest q = 0.0522` in MSSM oligodendrocytes; `0.1007` in PsychAD `EN_L3_5_IT_3`; `final-plos-one.tex#L191`).
2. **Mouse whole-profile Hellinger drift**: Strong against `young_1_3m` (`+0.1027` per `0.4 y` unadjusted `p = 4.14e-6`; `+0.0700` linear log-UMI `p = 1.54e-5`), stronger from `1m_only` (`+0.1263` unadjusted, `+0.0894` linear log-UMI) than `3m_only` (`+0.0981` unadjusted, `+0.0629` linear log-UMI), and **collapses when referenced to `18m`** (`+0.0072, p = 0.6656` linear log-UMI cell-level; `-0.0142, p = 0.6824` pseudobulk; `final-plos-one.tex#L204-L206`, [reviewer-rev2-hellinger.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/reviewer-rev2-hellinger.csv)). **Crucial methodological finding uncovered by this audit**: the existing `mouse_ref_18m` arm in [reviewer-rev2-hellinger-donors.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/reviewer-rev2-hellinger-donors.csv) still regresses across **all 23 mice (`1m to 30m`)** around an interior `18m` centroid rather than restricting the slope to `>= 18m` mice. Restricting directly to mature `>= 18m` mice (addressed separately in Item 6) is required to isolate true mature-to-old drift.
3. **Cell-type composition (`CLR`)**: Full-span analyses (`1m to 30m` mouse, `>= 20 y` human) yielded `15` global BH survivors (`10` mouse, `5` human). Under mature-only refits (`>= 18m` mouse, `>= 30y` human brain/lung; `final-plos-one.tex#L117-L124, L176-L183`, [mature-only-composition-verdicts.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/mature-only-composition-verdicts.csv)), all `5` human survivors hold (`q_orig_family = 0.0118 to 0.0279`, though human lung club and multiciliated cells still fail within-study meta-analysis), `1` mouse shift (`Bladder`, `n = 4` mature mice) is untestable, and all `9` remaining mouse shifts lose significance (`DIES`) with `9/9` preserving direction (`median 0.89x` slope) and `3.28x` median SE inflation (`1.30x to 5.53x`). Three secondary hump-shaped or adult-strengthened shifts (`Mouse Limb_Muscle` macrophage `-0.1429` and MSC `-0.1005`; `Human lung` cDC `+0.0229`) emerge only in the mature-only subset.
4. **Mouse lung disorder (`1m to 30m` only; no mature-only entropy run in manuscript)**: Off-program markers are defined relative to `1m and 3m` young lung profiles (`scripts/run_h3_transcriptional_disorder.py#L210-L212`) and regressed across `1m to 30m` (`+0.1743` per `15 months, SE = 0.2487, p = 0.4940`, [reviewer-phase2-depth-global.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/reviewer-phase2-depth-global.csv)); full-depth and rarefied Shannon entropy (`-0.0906` to `+0.0080`, `p = 0.68 to 0.97`) also span `1m to 30m`, with no mature-only (`>= 18m`) entropy fit reported in `final-plos-one.tex` (addressed in Item 4).
5. **Exploratory cross-species analyses**: Fruit fly (`AFCA`) already excludes Day 5 young adults (`30, 50, 70 days`; vector drift `-0.0467, p = 0.7958`, composition `12 + 14` global hits added in Item 1, concordance `+0.0951, p = 0.001`). By contrast, `C. elegans` (Gao `GSE229022`, `1, 6, 12, 14 days`) loses genome-wide statistical significance for vector drift once moved from a Day 1 young-adult reference (`+2.0134 unadj p = 3.80e-4`; `+1.5956 adj p = 4.87e-5`) to a **Day 6 adult reference** (`+1.0054 unadj p = 0.0526`; `+0.3853 adj p = 0.1001`; somatic-only Day 6 `+0.2338 adj p = 0.4183`). Naked mole-rat compares only `1.1 y` vs `11.3 y` (`n = 5` animals, < 40% of its `~31 y` lifespan). Zebrafish testis compares `5 to 27m` / `5 to 22m` (`n = 10` / `6` fish) and is strongly confounded by germline cell share (`97.23%` in the 6-fish fine set; `74.39%` in 10-fish broad pool).
6. **Human interneuron two-cohort replication (`381` genes: `77 VIP, 65 pvalb, 239 sst`)**: Restricted to adult donors (`age >= 20 y`, `MSSM_DLPFC` + `HBCC_DLPFC`; `scripts/run_rev2_de_interneuron.py#L14-L15`, `final-plos-one.tex#L146-L149, L222-L233`). Does NOT mix pediatric `0.2 to 19 y` HBCC/PsychAD donors into the slope fit.

## 3. Deep Audit of Critical Young-vs-Mature Boundaries and Conflations

### 3.1 Signature Derivation (`P1_Clean`) Is Purely `3m vs 18m`
- In `final-plos-one.tex#L107-L114`, mouse differential expression introduces the TMS cohort (`1, 3, 18, 21, 24, 30 months`) and then defines the candidate aging program (`P1_Clean = 139` genes, `P0_Raw257 = 257` genes) strictly from units comparing **`3 months` and `18 months`** across `Lung` and `Kidney` (`17` eligible units in [m2-unit-eligibility.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/m2-unit-eligibility.csv)).
- **Implication**: `P1_Clean` does not incorporate any `18m to 30m` late-life differential expression. Moreover, in human scoring (`Table 2`), pediatric donors (`2 to 19 y`) were present in `HBCC_DLPFC` (`2 to 85`), `Lung_Integrated` (`2 to 81`), `Lung_CellRef` (`12 to 76`), `Kidney_SN_v15` (`14 to 76`), and `PsychAD` (`0.2 to 97`, used via `DLPFC_Lifespan_Atlas`), which makes the failure of `P1_Clean` to outperform random matched gene sets (`q >= 0.0522`) even stronger as a negative result: even when allowed to include young-to-old gradients in human, the mouse `3m vs 18m` gene set did not generalize above random expression-matched sets.

### 3.2 Mouse Whole-Profile Hellinger Drift & the `18m` Midpoint Regression Issue
- Against `young_1_3m`, mouse global drift is large and highly significant across all depth adjustments and both cell-level and pseudobulk metrics (`+0.1027 unadjusted, p = 4.14e-6`; `+0.0700 linear_log_umi, p = 1.54e-5`; pseudobulk `+0.1576, p = 1.88e-6`).
- Comparing `1m_only` (`+0.1263 unadjusted`, `+0.0894 linear log-UMI`, `50` units) against `3m_only` (`+0.0981 unadjusted`, `+0.0629 linear log-UMI`, `59` units) shows roughly a `22 to 30%` larger slope when `1m` juveniles anchor the reference.
- Against the `18m` reference centroid (`68` units), `final-plos-one.tex#L204-L206` reports the collapsed depth-adjusted slope (`+0.0072, p = 0.67`) and negative pseudobulk slopes (`-0.010 to -0.018`). **Audit detail**: inspecting [reviewer-rev2-hellinger-donors.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/reviewer-rev2-hellinger-donors.csv) confirms that the `mouse_ref_18m` table retains all `23` mice (`0.0833 y` to `2.50 y`, i.e., `1m to 30m`). Because distance from an interior `18m` centroid increases toward both extremes (`1m/3m` on the young side and `24m/30m` on the old side), a linear regression across `1m to 30m` averages negative young-side slopes with positive old-side slopes. Isolating `age >= 18m` mice directly tests whether profiles move away from `18m` during true aging (`18m -> 30m`), which is evaluated separately in Item 6.

### 3.3 Composition (`CLR`) Power Loss vs Biological Disappearance
- Mouse primary `CLR` (`1m to 30m`) vs mature-only `CLR` (`18m to 30m`, `12-month` range vs `29-month` range) demonstrates why naive significance thresholding misleads if sample size and age spread are ignored:
  - In [mature-only-composition-verdicts.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/mature-only-composition-verdicts.csv), all `9` testable mouse survivors retain their original sign (`8/9` keep `0.81x to 2.60x` of full-span effect sizes), while their SEs expand by `1.30x to 5.53x` (median `3.28x`).
  - Conversely, Human brain (`MSSM_DLPFC`: `213` adult `>=20y` -> `209` mature `>=30y`, SE ratio `1.08x to 1.10x`) and pooled Lung (`119` -> `91`, SE ratio `1.32x to 1.33x`) retain nearly full leverage and remain significant (`q = 0.0118 to 0.0279`), and two hump-shaped mouse `Limb_Muscle` shifts (`macrophage` and `MSC`) only appear once `1m to 18m` trajectories are excluded.

### 3.4 `C. elegans` (Gao `GSE229022`): Strong Sensitivity to Day 1 Young-Adult Reference
- With Day 1 as baseline ([extended-worm-vector-global.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/extended-worm-vector-global.csv)), global profile drift per `14` days is `+2.0134` (`SE = 0.3998, p = 3.80e-4` unadjusted) and `+1.5956` (`SE = 0.2481, p = 4.87e-5` depth-adjusted).
- Replacing Day 1 with **Day 6 adult reference** ([extended-worm-vector-reference6-global.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/extended-worm-vector-reference6-global.csv), excluding the Day 1 -> Day 6 reproductive maturation shift) drops the unadjusted slope to `+1.0054` (`SE = 0.4629, p = 0.0526`) and the depth-adjusted slope to `+0.3853` (`SE = 0.2147, p = 0.1001`; somatic-only Day 6 ref `+0.2338, p = 0.4183`).
- Meanwhile, within-species gene-effect concordance (`+0.2640, joint p = 0.003` in Worm) and Gao composition shifts (`Item 1`: `ARSC +0.2160/day`, `Spermatheca -0.1366/day`) still include Day 1 (`1, 6, 12, 14 days`, `12` pools).

### 3.5 What Is Already Disclosed vs Remaining Gaps in `final-plos-one.tex`
- **Already cleanly disclosed in the frozen manuscript**:
  - Asymmetric age definitions between Mouse `18m` (middle age) and Human `30y` (cortical completion) (`#L123`, `#L177`).
  - Collapse of mouse young-ref vector drift under the `18m` reference (`#L37`, `#L204-L206`, `#L279`, `#L292`).
  - Power-loss interpretation of the `9` mouse mature-only composition `DIES` verdicts (`#L180-L183`, `#L300`).
  - Fly Day 5 exclusion (`#L82`, `#L272`) and Worm Day 1 vs Day 6 sensitivity (`#L274`).
  - Adult-only (`>= 20 y`) restriction in human composition, vectors, spline checks, and interneuron replication (`#L121`, `#L128`, `#L142`, `#L146`, `#L263`).
- **Remaining analytical gaps verified here (handled outside the frozen manuscript in Items 4, 6, 7, 8)**:
  1. Mouse lung entropy in `#L234-L260` has no mature-only (`>= 18m`) run in the manuscript -> **Item 4**.
  2. Mouse `18m`-referenced Hellinger slopes in [reviewer-rev2-hellinger.csv](file:///home/reza/projects/agescape-revision/results/reviewer-rev2-hellinger.csv) still fit across `1m to 30m` rather than strictly `age >= 18m` -> **Item 6**.
  3. Cross-species concordance in Mouse (`1-30m`) and Worm (`1-14d`) still includes pre-mature young animals (`1m/3m`, `Day 1`), and NMR covers only `1.1 vs 11.3 y` (`n = 5`).

Of 163 tissue-cell-type units tested, aging is powered in 17, all in lung and kidney.

Aging signal is powered in only 2 of 16 tissues

Of 163 tissue by cell-type units evaluated, 17 met the pre-registered mouse eligibility floor of at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells per group for the 3 month vs 18 month contrast. All 17 are in Lung or Kidney. The other 146 units, spread across the other 14 tissues, did not meet the floor, so this atlas cannot support an aging call for them.

Why so few? The sex by age design constraint

The eligibility floor was pre-registered to prevent spurious discoveries. In the 10x Chromium droplet arm of Tabula Muris Senis, only 17 units have at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells per group for the 3 month vs 18 month contrast.

All 3-month mice are male; all 18-month mice are female. The 3m vs 18m contrast is the only age comparison with multi-mouse replication in both groups.

Statistical guards: Permutation null, sex-confound caveat, and trajectory independence

0. Sex Confound (structural): The 3m vs 18m contrast compares male-young against female-old mice; age and sex are fully confounded by design in the droplet arm (G4). The permutation null bounds the false-positive rate but cannot deconfound sex, so every aging call on this page is conditional on the TMS design.

1. Marker Validation: Canonical senescence markers (Cdkn2a, Spp1, Ccl2) showed positive direction but did not all reach cell-level significance individually; the claim rests on the 12-fold permutation margin.

2. Differentiation Guard (G5): Guard check, Marrow: diffusion pseudotime vs chronological age, Spearman rho 0.016. Pseudotime here tracks cell maturation, not age, so maturation structure does not confound the age comparisons. No trajectory-based aging claim is made.

Housekeeping guard: Ribosomal and mitochondrial transcript inflation

Ribosomal (Rpl/Rps) and mitochondrial (mt-) genes represent a massive fraction of raw single-cell sequencing reads. Across the 17 units, housekeeping genes account for only 1.7% of background genes, yet constitute up to 14% of naive DE hits if uncorrected. All reported programs filter housekeeping inflation.

Savina et al. 2022 (Aging) reproduction: 29 DE genes at author threshold (paper reported 0)

Savina et al. 2022 (Aging) reported zero global DE genes between young and aged naked mole-rat epidermis at their stated rule. We do not reproduce the literal zero: at p less than 0.05 and absolute log2FC at least 1.0 we find 29 genes. The most parsimonious explanation is a units ambiguity, since the paper's threshold appears to be in natural-log units, equivalent to about absolute log2FC 1.44; at cutoff 1.45 we find 9 genes (p rule) and 6 (FDR rule). The paper's broader claim, that naked mole-rat epidermis is unusually stable with age, is partially reproduced. Two caveats: Igfbp3, the paper's key gene, is absent from the GEO count file and could not be checked; our QC retained 10,251 cells vs the paper's 10,232.

2. SenNet Query Transparency

A query of 6,820 SenNet public datasets on 2026-10-14 confirmed that no public scRNA-seq datasets currently index donor ages in the unrestricted public API. The human cross-species arm relies on Tabula Sapiens release 1.0 and is explicitly flagged as UNTESTABLE / LOW-POWERED due to donor count constraints (n = 4).

3. Eight-Line Pipeline Summary

  1. Quality control applied per-tissue (mitochondrial fraction, min/max genes, Scrublet doublet removal).
  2. 163 tissue by cell-type units evaluated; 17 met the mouse eligibility floor (at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells in 3m and 18m, all in Lung or Kidney; 146 ineligible across the other 14 tissues).
  3. Pseudobulk differential expression per tissue by cell-type unit (PyDESeq2, 3m vs 18m, design ~ sex + age_group) with patient block bootstrap CIs.
  4. Permutation null calibration on the top unit (Lung adventitial cell) yields 4.6 genes vs 55 observed; the within-3m sex control is not estimable (all-male cohort) and contributes a structural 0.
  5. Guard check on Marrow: diffusion pseudotime vs chronological age shows Spearman rho 0.016. Pseudotime tracks cell maturation, not age; no trajectory-based aging claim is made.
  6. Ensembl 1:1 reciprocal orthologs mapped for mouse-human and mouse-NMR.
  7. Housekeeping inflation guard monitors ribosomal and mitochondrial fraction against 0.017 background.
  8. Life-stage normalized regression and Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) evaluate NMR equivalence (not established, p = 0.329).

4. Three Load-Bearing Limitations

  • Sex by Age Droplet Confounding: In the TMS droplet dataset, 1m, 24m, and 30m animals are male-only and 21m animals are female-only, so the 3m vs 18m contrast is sex-confounded (male-young vs female-old). Primary models rely on that replicated contrast or continuous covariates, and no analysis here can deconfound sex from age in this arm.
  • Outlier Scope: Naked mole-rat skin data represents young and aged animals (Savina et al. 2022, Aging). Multi-tissue in vivo single-cell atlases across a 30-year span do not exist.
  • Cross-Sectional Sampling: All datasets reflect cross-sectional donor cohorts, not longitudinal tracking of living individuals.

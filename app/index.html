Interactive single-cell aging atlas

Aging is not one program. Here is the evidence.

We searched every cell type in the aging mouse atlas for genes that change with age. The signal is real but rare: only 17 of 163 cell-type units have enough mice and cells to test; 14 of the 17 show aging, all in lung and kidney, and each does so through largely its own genes. In humans, tested in 621 public donors and in a 284-donor lifespan atlas, the program does not rise as a whole. This page shows the evidence, and the exact study design that would settle the rest.

The story in 30 seconds

  1. 1 Where can aging even be measured? Only 17 of 163 mouse cell-type units have enough mice and cells to test. All 17 are in lung and kidney.
  2. 2 Is the signal real? Yes. The top unit shows 55 aging genes against about 5 expected by chance, and the results match the original published atlas.
  3. 3 What is the aging program? 261 genes change with age, but most act in only one cell type. Aging looks private, not universal.
  4. 4 Does it hold across species? Mouse: clearly yes. Naked mole-rat: flat, unproven. Human: tested in 621 donors, the program does not rise as a whole; in the 284-donor lifespan atlas, glia rise and immune cells fall.
  5. 5 What would answer the human question? A cohort with enough donors spread across ages. The power map shows how many, and the last graph shows the 284-donor lifespan cohort that already ran it.
Chapter 1

Of 163 tissue-cell-type units, 17 are testable and 14 show aging, all in lung and kidney.

“Powered” means at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells in both the young and old groups: the minimum needed to detect aging without fooling ourselves.

Aging signal is powered in only 2 of 16 tissues

Of 163 tissue by cell-type units evaluated, 17 met the pre-registered mouse eligibility floor of at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells per group for the 3 month vs 18 month contrast. All 17 are in lung or kidney. The other 146 units, spread across the other 14 tissues, did not meet the floor, so this atlas cannot support an aging call for them. Solid tiles: testable units. Hollow tiles: not testable, so no aging call is made.

Chapter 2

Top signal is 12x its permutation null, and pipeline agrees with original paper.

Three sanity checks before believing any number: the signal must beat a shuffled-label null, match the published paper, and be independent of cell maturity.

Pipeline direction matches the published atlas in 13 of 17 units (4 partial, 0 disagree)

Agreement with the TMS paper's published age coefficients across the 17 powered units: 13 agree, 4 partial, 0 disagree. The strongest unit, Kidney proximal convoluted tubule, shows Spearman rho 0.866 over 13,019 shared genes, with sign concordance 0.977 among the paper's significant genes. For 15 of 17 units the comparison is between our dichotomous 3 month vs 18 month effects and the paper's continuous-age coefficients; a continuous-age bridge model was fit for 2 units, and the agreement table states which comparator applies per unit.

Agreement status across all 17 powered units: 13 agree, 4 partial, 0 disagree

Calibration: 55 age-differentially expressed genes observed, 5 expected by chance, 0 differentially expressed by sex within the 3-month group

Observed in the top unit (lung adventitial cell), 3 month vs 18 month: 55 age-differentially expressed genes. Label-permutation null, mean: 4.6. Differential expression by sex within the 3-month group (2 female vs 5 male mice): 0 genes at FDR < 0.05, so this unit's aging signal is not driven by sex.

Statistical guards: Permutation null, sex-confound caveat, and trajectory independence

0. Sex by age (partial): 1-month, 24-month, and 30-month groups are male-only and the 21-month group is female-only, so sex and age cannot be separated across the full design (G4). The 3m vs 18m contrast has both sexes in both arms (5 male + 2 female vs 2 male + 2 female), the primary model adjusts for sex, and the within-3-month sex comparison finds nothing in this unit. Aging calls on this page are still conditional on the TMS design.

1. Marker Validation: Spp1 and Ccl2 move in the expected direction where they appear but do not reach cell-level significance individually; the aging call rests on the 12-fold permutation margin.

2. Differentiation Guard (G5): Guard check, Marrow: diffusion pseudotime vs chronological age, Spearman rho 0.016. Pseudotime here tracks cell maturation, not age, so maturation structure does not confound the age comparisons. No trajectory-based aging claim is made.

Housekeeping guard: Ribosomal and mitochondrial transcript inflation

Ribosomal (Rpl/Rps) and mitochondrial (mt-) genes represent a large fraction of raw single-cell sequencing reads. Across the 17 units, housekeeping genes account for only 1.7% of background genes, yet still make up as much as 10% of one unit's program after standard filtering (kidney fenestrated cell). All reported programs filter ribosomal and mitochondrial inflation.

Savina et al. 2022 (Aging) reproduction: 29 DE genes at author threshold (paper reported 0)

Savina et al. 2022 (Aging) reported zero global DE genes between young and aged naked mole-rat epidermis at their stated rule. We do not reproduce the literal zero: at p less than 0.05 and absolute log2FC at least 1.0 we find 29 genes. The most parsimonious explanation is a units ambiguity, since the paper's threshold appears to be in natural-log units, equivalent to about absolute log2FC 1.44; at cutoff 1.45 we find 9 genes (p rule) and 6 (FDR rule). The paper's broader claim, that naked mole-rat epidermis is unusually stable with age, is partially reproduced. Two caveats: Igfbp3, the paper's key gene, is absent from the GEO count file and could not be checked; our QC retained 10,251 cells vs the paper's 10,232.

What would settle the human question?

At donor-age SD 12 years and dispersion 0.1, the minimum detectable 30-year log2FC first falls to 0.491 at 16 donors (values from the power simulation shown in G13). At dispersion 0.2, reaching a comparable detectable value (0.521) requires 16 donors at SD 16 years. At dispersion 0.4, detecting a 30-year log2FC of 0.556 requires 50 donors at SD 12 years. The DLPFC Lifespan Atlas in G16 is that design, already run: 284 donors, age SD 24.

2. Human Data Coverage

The human arm is tested in 11 public CELLxGENE cohorts (621 control donors) and the 284-donor DLPFC Lifespan Atlas under a data use agreement. A query of the SenNet public index found no unrestricted datasets with donor ages. The Tabula Sapiens skin panel (n = 4) is kept only as the low-powered design that motivates Chapter 5.

3. Ten-Step Pipeline Summary

  1. Quality control applied per-tissue (mitochondrial fraction, min/max genes, Scrublet doublet removal).
  2. 163 tissue by cell-type units evaluated; 17 met the mouse eligibility floor (at least 3 mice and at least 50 cells in 3m and 18m, all in Lung or Kidney; 146 ineligible across the other 14 tissues); 14 of the 17 show aging.
  3. Pseudobulk differential expression per tissue by cell-type unit (PyDESeq2, 3m vs 18m, design ~ sex + age_group).
  4. Permutation null calibration on the top unit (Lung adventitial cell) yields 4.6 genes vs 55 observed; the within-3-month sex comparison (5 male vs 2 female mice) finds 0 sex-linked genes.
  5. Guard check on Marrow: diffusion pseudotime vs chronological age shows Spearman rho 0.016. Pseudotime tracks cell maturation, not age; no trajectory-based aging claim is made.
  6. Ensembl 1:1 reciprocal orthologs mapped for mouse-human and mouse-NMR.
  7. Housekeeping inflation guard monitors ribosomal and mitochondrial fraction against 0.017 background.
  8. Life-stage normalized regression and Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) evaluate NMR equivalence (not established, p = 0.329).
  9. M9 human cohort arm: the 261-gene program scored in 621 control donors across 11 public CELLxGENE cohorts (brain, kidney, lung); the whole-donor score does not rise with age in any cohort, and cell-type directions conflict across cohorts.
  10. M10 lifespan-atlas arm (G16): the same program scored in the DLPFC Lifespan Atlas (284 control donors aged 0.2 to 97, SD 24, under AD Knowledge Portal data use agreement) using the atlas authors' per-donor, per-cell-type expression summaries. Cortex-wide average is flat (p = 0.19); astrocytes and oligodendrocytes rise and perivascular macrophages fall at Bonferroni level in both raw-sum and CPM schemes (7 cell types concordant total); normalization-dependent cell types are drawn hollow and claimed as no direction. Regression aggregates only; per-donor values are controlled data.

4. Three Load-Bearing Limitations

  • Partial sex by age confounding: In the TMS droplet arm, 1-month, 24-month, and 30-month mice are male-only and 21-month mice are female-only; only the 3-month and 18-month groups include both sexes. The primary 3m vs 18m contrast therefore has both-sex replication and adjusts for sex, but sex and age cannot be separated across the full age range.
  • Outlier Scope: Naked mole-rat skin data represents young and aged animals (Savina et al. 2022, Aging). Multi-tissue in vivo single-cell atlases across a 30-year span do not exist.
  • Cross-Sectional Sampling: All datasets reflect cross-sectional donor cohorts, not longitudinal tracking of living individuals.

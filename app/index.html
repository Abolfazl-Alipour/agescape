⚠️ Critical Confound: Sex × Age Droplet Confounding

In the 10x Chromium droplet arm of Tabula Muris Senis, all 3-month animals are male, while all 18-month and 24-month animals are female. This creates complete statistical confounding between sex and age in uncorrected naive contrasts. In this explorer, all reported primary differential expression models strictly utilize the pre-registered M18m vs M24m or within-sex models, and sex-floor calibration confirmed 0 DE genes at baseline.

1. Cross-Sectional Sampling vs Longitudinal Aging

All human, mouse, and naked mole-rat datasets analyzed are cross-sectional cohorts of distinct biological individuals sacrificed at specific ages. They do not represent longitudinal single-cell tracking of individual organisms over time. Apparent transcriptomic shifts may reflect cohort effects, survivorship selection, or environmental variance.

2. Naked Mole-Rat Tissue & Culturing Limitations

The Heterocephalus glaber single-cell dataset (Savina et al. 2022 GEO GSE152886) comprises cultured primary dermal skin fibroblasts across three age brackets (young 3-4y, middle 11-14y, old 21-24y). Single-cell multi-tissue atlas data from wild or captive naked mole-rats across an entire 30-year in vivo lifespan does not currently exist in the public domain. In vitro culture adaptation may partially mask in vivo systemic aging signals.

3. Human Statistical Power & Donor Age Dispersion

Human cohorts with fewer than 6 donors or age standard deviation below 12 years are mathematically unpowered to detect subtle transcriptional aging slopes. Per our pre-registered methodology (§4.5 rule 6), such cell types are classified as Low-Powered rather than biological divergence.

4. Technical Dropouts & Housekeeping Artifacts

High single-cell technical dropouts in 10x 3' sequencing artificially suppress detection of low-abundance transcripts. Furthermore, naive cross-species correlation is heavily distorted by ubiquitous ribosomal (Rpl/Rps) and mitochondrial (mt-) genes. Our analysis implements a strict housekeeping inflation guard, demonstrating that true biological conservation persists even when housekeeping transcripts are excluded.

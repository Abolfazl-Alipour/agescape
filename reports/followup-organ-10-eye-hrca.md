# Dataset 10: Human Retina Cell Atlas (HRCA) Integration (Li et al., Nat Genet 2026, PMC13050529)

## Verdict
- **Zero positive mouse `P1_Clean` signature transport across all `9` tested snRNA-seq and scRNA-seq suites (`2,000` matched-null draws per cell group; [P1 Signature](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-p1-signature.csv))**:
  - In adult unbiased single-region snRNA-seq (`Sn_Adult_MacularOnly`, `n = 86`, ages `18 to 90` yr; `Sn_Adult_PeripheralOnly`, `n = 50`, ages `21 to 90` yr), peripheral retina showed `0 / 9` significant classes (`min q_BH_composite = 0.2960`, `min q_BH_empirical = 0.3272`), whereas macular retina showed significant composite shifts in `MG` (`-0.0492` per decade, `q_BH_composite = 0.0324`, `q_BH_empirical = 0.0022`) and `RGC` (`-0.0379` per decade, `q_BH_composite = 0.0324`, `q_BH_empirical = 0.0090`), BOTH strictly in the **negative (opposite)** direction to mouse aging (`P1_Clean` UP genes declined with age: `MG` `up_effect = -0.0695, p = 0.0064`; `RGC` `up_effect = -0.0616, p = 0.0026`).
  - Negative `RGC` slopes (`-0.0337` to `-0.0487` per decade) persisted in `Sn_Adult_Unenriched` (`q_BH_empirical = 0.0180`), `Sn_Adult_ExactAge` (`q_BH_empirical = 0.0135`), single-cohort `Sn_ChenAncestry_Adult` (`n = 51`, `q_BH_composite = 0.0132`, `q_BH_empirical = 0.0135`), and `Sn_AllAges_Unenriched` (`n = 92`, `q_BH_composite = 0.000685`).
  - Across all `3` unbiased scRNA-seq suites (`Sc15_StudyAdj` `n = 15`, `Sc9_External_StudyAdj` `n = 9`, `Sc5_External_NoDisease` `n = 5`), `0` cell classes passed `q_BH < 0.05` on either analytic or empirical criteria (`min q_BH_empirical = 0.1659 to 0.7088`).
- **Zero cross-cell-type directional concordance across all `9` suites (`perm_p_one_sided = 0.0749 to 0.8042`; [Concordance](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-concordance.csv)).**
- **Region stratification resolves apparent compositional shifts and highlights macular vs peripheral divergence ([Fractions](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-fractions.csv), [Gene Summary](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-gene-summary.csv))**:
  - In pure single-region adult cohorts (`Sn_Adult_MacularOnly` `n = 86`, `Sn_Adult_PeripheralOnly` `n = 50`), `0` cell classes passed Family A CLR BH correction (`min q_BH_family_a = 0.1612` and `0.1606`). Apparent CLR shifts (`AC` and `RGC`) in pooled `Sn_Adult_Unenriched` (`q_BH = 0.0024` and `0.0147`) and `Sn_ChenAncestry_Adult` (`BC, AC, HC`, `q_BH = 0.0461`) reflect linear residual variance when macular and peripheral samples are combined.
  - Family B donor-level OLS detected `0` global hits (`q_BH < 0.05`) in `Sn_Adult_PeripheralOnly` (`M_B = 145,223`) and all `3` scRNA-seq suites (`M_B = 67,446 to 114,446`), vs `74` hits in `Sn_Adult_MacularOnly` (`M_B = 145,355`, `51` in `RGC`, `9` in `AC`, `6` in `Microglia`, `3` in `Astrocyte`, `2` in `Cone`, `2` in `HC`, `1` in `MG`), `4` in `Sn_Adult_Unenriched` (`RGC: VRK2, MAP4K3-DT, ENSG00000283098`; `HC: FGF10`), `4` in `Sn_Adult_ExactAge`, and `5` in `Sn_ChenAncestry_Adult` (led by X-linked `AC: PLS3`, `effect = -0.1903/dec`, `p = 1.81e-09`, `q_BH = 0.000265`). Adding the `7` pediatric donors (`3 to 17` yr) in `Sn_AllAges_Unenriched` (`n = 93`) inflated global hits from `4` to `1,500`, isolating strong childhood developmental maturation.

## 1. Primary Source Verification and Integration Overlap Audit
- **Publication and Portal**: Li et al., *Nature Genetics* 2026 (`DOI: 10.1038/s41588-025-02454-1`, `PMID: 41578023`, `PMCID: PMC13050529`). Paper reports ~3.9 million cells across 125 donors (combining 8 published cohorts and ~2.7 million previously unpublished nuclei).
- **CELLxGENE Collection `4c6eaf5c-6d57-4c76-b1e9-60df8c655f1e` ("Single cell atlas of the human retina")**: Contains `8` datasets; only the two non-redundant all-cell objects (`d6505c89-c43d-4c28-8c4f-7351a5fd5528` snRNA-seq all cells, `3,177,310` nuclei across `104` donor entries; `be41a86a-b606-4b1c-8055-32f334898775` scRNA-seq all cells, `265,767` cells across `20` donors) were analyzed ([Donor Audit](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-donor-audit.csv)). The remaining 6 objects are class-level subsets of these two objects.
- **Unbiased Cell Filtering**: Restricted strictly to `suspension_enriched_cell_types == "na"` and `suspension_depleted_cell_types == "na"`, excluding all `Chen_rgc` NeuN-enriched libraries (`1,294,028` snRNA nuclei) and all `Sanes_GSE148077` CD73-rod-depleted / CD90-RGC-enriched peripheral libraries (`18,683` scRNA cells). All counts in `raw/X` are verified exact non-negative integers mapped across `35,466` unique HGNC/Ensembl symbols after collapsing duplicate symbols by sum.
- **Within-Donor Cross-Study Pairing Audit**: Across the `3,177,310` snRNA nuclei, `(donor_id, regiongroup)` maps uniquely (`1:1`) to a single `study_name` for all unbiased nuclei, whereas `18` paired donors (`19-D005`, `19-D008`, `19-D009`, `Chen_19_D003`, `Chen_19_D006`, `Chen_19_D007`, `Chen_19_D010`, `Chen_19_D011`, `Chen_19_D019`, `Chen_D005_13`, `Chen_D009_13`, `Chen_D013_13`, `Chen_D019_13`, `Chen_D021_13`, `Chen_D026_13`, `Chen_D027_13`, `Chen_D028_13`, `Chen_D030_13`) contribute `macular` nuclei in `Chen_c_GSE247157` (`n = 20` adult macular total; `Chen_a` has `0` macular nuclei) and `peripheral` nuclei in `Chen_a` (`n = 18` adult peripheral total). `study` was assigned per `(donor_id, regiongroup)` so macular-only (`Sn_Adult_MacularOnly`) and peripheral-only (`Sn_Adult_PeripheralOnly`) models have zero within-donor study mixing and separate `Chen_a+Chen_c_GSE247157` indicators are used when pooling regions ([Donors](../results/followup-organ-10-eye-hrca-donors.csv)).

## 2. Explicit Multiple-Testing Families
- **Inferential unit**: Unique donor (`n = 51 to 93` across snRNA suites; `n = 5 to 15` across scRNA suites). Age modeled as continuous `age_per_decade = age / 10`.
- **Family A (CLR composition)**: `9 to 10` major classes (`Sn_*`) or `4 to 8` classes (`Sc_*`), BH across `K_A` classes per suite (`min_donors = 15..20` in `Sn`, `5..6` in `Sc`; `log_lib` excluded from CLR predictors; `disease_any` adjusted in `Sc15` and `Sc9`).
- **Family B (Donor-level gene age OLS + concordance)**: Genes with `CPM >= 1.0` in `>= 50%` of eligible donors (`>= 10` cells per donor-class), global BH across all `M_B` (`cell_type, gene`) tests per suite (`M_B = 145,223 to 147,819` in `Sn`; `67,446 to 114,446` in `Sc`); `min_residual_df = 5` in `Sn`, `3` in `Sc15/Sc9`, `2` in `Sc5_External_NoDisease`. Covariates: `study` dummies + `sex_male` + `log_lib` (+ `frac_peripheral` in mixed-region suites; + `disease_any` in `Sc15`/`Sc9`). Concordance: mean pairwise Pearson `r` across class `t`-statistics with `1,000` donor-age permutations.
- **Family C (`P1_Clean` mouse signature)**: `76` UP / `42` DOWN mapped human genes, analytic composite OLS + `2,000` expression-decile-matched random draws (`matched_null_effect_pvalue`), BH across `K_C` tested cell classes per suite.

## 4. Key Limitations
1. **Macular sub-region heterogeneity**: `MACULAR` combines `fovea centralis`, `macula lutea proper`, and `macula lutea`; fovea-to-parafovea anatomical sampling ratios can influence macular RGC and glial variance across `Chen_ancestry` and `Chen_b_GSE226108`, whereas `Chen_c_GSE247157` and `Shekhar_GSE237204` sampled `macula lutea`.
2. **Topcoded >89 ages**: Five adult snRNA donors are topcoded at `>89 years` (coded `90`); `Sn_Adult_ExactAge` (`n = 81`) removes them and confirms consistent results.
3. **scRNA disease and Organ 07 duplication**: `10 / 15` scRNA donors have ocular or systemic diseases (`6` `Sanes` donors duplicate Organ 07 `GSE148077`, `3` `Scheetz` ocular diseases, `1` `Wong` cataract), leaving only `5` disease-free external scRNA donors (`Sc5_External_NoDisease`).
4. **snRNA study overlap (`Chen_a` and `Chen_c_GSE247157`)**: Eighteen donors contribute paired peripheral nuclei via `Chen_a` and macular nuclei via `Chen_c_GSE247157`, motivating single-region primary stratification (`Sn_Adult_MacularOnly` and `Sn_Adult_PeripheralOnly`).

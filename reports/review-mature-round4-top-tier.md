## Verdict: REJECT at top-tier standard

This is a careful, unusually honest constraint paper. It is not a Nature Aging /
Nature / Cell paper. Its central claim is a null result against a construct the
authors invented, tested with a probe the paper itself shows is unstable,
sex-confounded, and unvalidated. Its positive findings are real but small:
five fraction shifts in two human organs, and per-cell narrowing effects of
0.02 to 0.07 standard deviations per decade that are nearly invisible in the
paper's own Figure 2. The paper would be a strong specialist-journal paper
(PLOS Biology, Nature Communications, eLife) after the MAJOR items below are
fixed. At top-tier standard it fails on the probe, on the construct, and on
replication, and no amount of reframing fixes that without new analysis.

What is genuinely good, so the verdict is not mistaken for carelessness:
donor-level inference throughout; the propeller independent check; the
batch-adjustment benchmark that cuts against the authors' own artifact story;
caveats stated in the Abstract (all-male old mice, 170 of 252 testable);
and a Discussion that names what was not tested instead of hiding it.
The problem is not honesty. The problem is that the design cannot carry
the headline at this venue.

## The eight questions, answered

1. **Novelty and significance.** "Local and plural, no uniform program,
cells narrow" is two different things stapled together. The narrowing is a
genuine reversal of a published prediction (expression gets less spread with
age, not more), but the effects are tiny, one of the five plotted slopes is
not significant (Gao Shannon p 0.054, Fig 2), and the direct published
contradiction it positions against (De Man lung entropy rise, ref 41) was
never tested in its own cohort because the Baylor lungs are blocked.
The "no uniform program" half is a null against a four-criterion construct
so stringent that no serious theory predicts it. A top-tier reader will
call this a null result dressed as a finding, and the dressing is the
construct.
2. **Story coherence.** The paper reads as a stitched list, not one study.
Methods Table 3 labels the analyses "Block 1" through "Block 10"
(tex lines 189-209); there is a "Follow-up cohort rules" subsection
(line 161); Appendix B states "Other rows retain their earlier verdicts
because those cohorts were not retested at 30-plus" (line 479); the
signature is defined midway through Results (line 261) after being used
in the Abstract and Methods; Appendix A carries a full parallel
young-to-old paper. The process shows through on every page.
3. **Figure quality.** Improved (serif, clean ticks, plain-word captions)
but still defective: Fig 1 donor-panel titles still overlap; Fig 2 has
duplicate panel letters and flat lines; S1 and S5 carry factual mislabels;
S2 plots incompatible units on one axis. Details under MAJOR/MINOR.
4. **Abstract and Introduction.** The abstract now has problem, gap,
approach, results, conclusion, and a reader who reads nothing else would
grasp the story. But it omits three caveats that change the story (heart
Shannon null, TRMT11 moderated-unverified and SEA-AD failure, GTEx
whole-blood positive). The Introduction is improved (hypothesis framing),
but still contains a "P1 analysis" mislabel (line 53) and a gap claim
("No prior study tested...") asserted without a systematic-review citation.
5. **Constraint framing.** The hedge swallows the positives. The construct
is defined so strictly that failing it is unsurprising, and the paper
concedes the live alternatives (pathway-level uniformity, modular
conserved signatures, transportable clocks) were never tested
(lines 53, 335, 387). The reader is left unsure whether the paper found
that aging is local, or merely that it is not identical everywhere.
6. **Caveats.** The all-male old mice and the 82 of 252 untestable units
are prominent (both in the Abstract, lines 31-33). The GTEx whole-blood
composition confounding and the moderated interneuron failure are in
Results, Limitations, and Conclusions but absent from the Abstract, and
the Conclusions mention the whole-blood positive without re-attaching
its caveat (line 401-403). A figure-only or abstract-only reader misses
both.
7. **Jargon.** The no-jargon rule reached figures and captions but not the
body: P1m 39x, P0m 16x, both-pass 24x, E1 22x, E2 23x, MDE80 13x,
Block 1-10 labels, and undefined codes M9/M10, LACA, HCATA, multihot.
Details under MINOR.
8. **Publishability and the single change.** REJECT. The single change
that would most improve the paper's chances is a positive control for
the transport test: held-out-mouse validation of the mature signature
plus a progeroid or accelerated-aging model, with the signature
re-derived with both sexes at old age. Without it, the central null is
uninterpretable, because the paper cannot distinguish "no uniform
program" from "broken probe". Everything else is repair; this is the
missing experiment.

## BLOCKER

1. **The central null rests on an unvalidated probe.** The mature
signature is 14x the size of the young-to-old signature for a cause
that "is not established" (lines 117, 265, 335); it derives from
kidney, lung, and marrow only (lines 115, 263); all four 30-month
mice are male (lines 115-121); no leave-one-donor-out check was ever
run on it (line 263); 1,212 of its 1,914 genes rest on a single unit
(Fig S1); and the paper concedes "transport failure cannot be assigned
cleanly to species divergence rather than the probe" (Discussion,
line 335). There is no positive control anywhere: no held-out-mouse
validation, no progeroid model, no demonstration that this pipeline
can detect a real transport signal in the predicted direction. The
only two both-test passes run negative. A null from an unvalidated,
sex-confounded probe is not a top-tier result. It is an uninterpretable
null.
2. **Outcome-dependent primary family.** The 624-test composition
family "was selected after both correction outcomes were available"
(lines 131-133, 225, 383). The only two associations that exist
because of that choice, mouse lung vein endothelial and human kidney
collecting duct, fail core-only q, total-cell adjustment, and
propeller (lines 225-227). Disclosure does not cure selection: at
top-tier standard the primary family is the 431-test family and the
624-test family is a sensitivity, or the authors must show the
conclusions do not depend on the choice. They do depend on it for
exactly the two promoted tests.
3. **The headline positive is positioned against a finding it never
tests.** The narrowing claim is framed against De Man and colleagues'
human lung entropy rise (ref 41). The Baylor lungs from that atlas
could not be tested for lack of public cell labels (lines 239-241,
399), and the substitute test used control lungs from an idiopathic
pulmonary fibrosis cohort, a different population. The paper's own
narrowing effects are 0.02 to 0.07 SD per decade against a spread of
roughly plus/minus 2 SD, visible as nearly flat fitted lines in
Fig 2, and the Gao scatter slope (minus 0.0723, p 0.041) is marginal.
A top-tier paper cannot headline a reversal of a published result
while leaving that result untested in its own cohort.
4. **The construct is a straw man.** The four criteria demand the same
direction in every cell type, overlapping gene sets, comparable
magnitude, and presence in each species (Introduction, lines 47-51).
No current theory predicts that. The paper concedes the live
alternatives were never tested: no pathway-level uniformity test, no
Tyshkovskiy clock benchmark, no Dobner gene-set test, no Buckley
clock benchmark (lines 53, 335, 387). The headline null therefore
rules out a criterion nobody holds while leaving the serious versions
of a shared program untested. At top-tier standard this is a
definitional victory, not an empirical one.

## MAJOR

1. **No independent replication of any headline positive.** The two
lung fraction shifts fail within-study replication: study-aware
meta-combination gives club-cell fixed effect minus 0.0061 (p 0.58)
and multiciliated plus 0.0098 (p 0.39), with no study-specific slope
significant (Results, line 231). The narrowing slopes have no
held-out cohort. The interneuron replication rests on one gene
(TRMT11), an unverified moderated test, and a failed third cohort
(SEA-AD, 15 donors; Results lines 257-259). Every positive in the
paper is either unreplicated or replicated once, weakly.
2. **The mouse arm is underpowered throughout.** Drift is estimated
on 14 mice aged 18 to 30 months (line 283); derivation on 4 old
males (lines 115-121); the fraction refit on 7 to 13 donors per
tissue (line 229). The paper itself says the nine mouse fraction
losses are power losses, not nulls. The cross-species design's mouse
half cannot carry the weight the conclusions put on it.
3. **Eligibility floors are unjustified and determinative.** Only 36
of 151 mouse units were eligible for derivation because the rest
"lacked at least three mice and 50 cells on both sides of the
contrast" (lines 115, 263). No rationale for 3 mice or 50 cells
appears anywhere in the tex, and no sensitivity analysis varies
them. The entire signature, and therefore every transport claim,
depends on an unexplained threshold.
4. **The 14-fold size gap is unexplained and uninvestigated.**
P1m (1,914 genes) is about fourteen times P1 (139 genes) "under the
same derivation rules, and the cause of the size difference is not
established" (lines 117, 265, 335). No power-matched comparison, no
age-span decomposition, no method-matched control is offered. A
reviewer cannot tell whether the mature signature is biology, a
power artifact of the wider gene pool at 30 months, or a sex
artifact of the all-male old group.
5. **Figure 2 overstates the narrowing and mislabels its panels.**
Five slopes are plotted; one is not significant (Gao Shannon,
p 0.0539, annotated in the figure). The fitted lines are visually
flat against clouds spanning roughly plus/minus 2 SD, so the figure
shows the reader a null while the caption calls it narrowing. Both
scatter panels are labeled "(a)" and both Shannon panels "(c)",
so the caption's references to "Panel (a)", "Panel (b)", "Panel (c)"
are ambiguous (Fig 2; caption lines 233-247). Relabel the panels
(a) through (e) or (a1)/(a2), and do not present a non-significant
slope as one of the finding panels without marking it null in the
figure itself.
6. **The narrowing evidence is not three independent findings.**
The 14 primary endpoints (Results, line 235) include several
transforms of the same within-cell spread: scatter, Shannon entropy,
KL methods, and KL square-root. Scatter, Shannon, and the E2 count
all measure whether expression concentrates on fewer genes; they
are not three independent lines of evidence for narrowing, they are
one measurement in three guises. The paper never tests or states
their dependence.
7. **The paper reads as a revision-response document, not a designed
study.** Methods Table 3 labels analyses "Block 1" through
"Block 10" (lines 189-209); there is a "Follow-up cohort rules"
subsection (line 161); Appendix B states "Other rows retain their
earlier verdicts because those cohorts were not retested at 30-plus"
(line 479); the signature is defined midway through Results
(line 261) after being used in the Abstract and Methods; Appendix A
carries a full parallel young-to-old paper including its own
transport passes. A top-tier reviewer will not accept workflow
labels and verdict bookkeeping as Methods.
8. **The one positive-direction transport is dismissed without being
tested.** GTEx whole blood is the one positive both-pass
(plus 0.0119 per decade bin, empirical q 0.0015; line 275), and the
paper states it "was not tested for composition confounding, so it
stands as a bulk-composition candidate, not as within-cell
transport." The thesis that there is no positive-direction pass
depends on an untested dismissal of the single positive result.
Run the deconvolution or composition adjustment; it is the most
important unresolved analysis in the paper, and it is listed as
unresolved in Limitations (line 387-389) instead of being done.
9. **Reproducibility links are stale.** Data Availability points to
tag mature-to-old-rewrite-2026-11-21 (lines 407, 411, 413) while
the manuscript of record is dd6f503, tag
mature-to-old-donor-scatterplots-2026-11-22. A reader following the
manifest, the source tree, or the archive links does not reach this
manuscript. At top-tier standard, reproducibility links that resolve
to an earlier version are a submission defect, not a typo.
10. **Two Supporting Information figures carry factual mislabels.**
Fig S1: "one test group is one tissue-by-species group" (tex
line 433; Fig S1 x-axis). The units are tissue-by-cell-type units
in mouse only; "species" is meaningless here, since derivation used
one species. Fig S5: "random-gene null" (tex line 441; Fig S5
legend). The Methods define a joint donor-permutation null that
assigns one permuted age to each donor (lines 143-145); it permutes
ages, not genes. Both mislabels are in the figure and its caption,
and both misstate the method.

## MINOR

1. **Fig 1 donor-panel titles still overlap.** "Lung multiciliated
epithelial" and "L6b glutamatergic neuron" run together in the
render (Fig 1, lower panels). This was flagged before the rebuild
and survives it. Shorten the titles or stagger the panels.
2. **Fig 4 panel titles are inconsistently cased.** "VIP
interneurons", "pvalb interneurons", "sst interneurons"
(Fig 4). Lowercase gene symbols as panel titles read as a
drafting error. The TRMT11 label also sits on top of the ring
cluster in the SST panel.
3. **Fig S2 plots incompatible units on one axis.** Mouse slopes are
per month and human slopes per year; the footnote admits it, but
the visual still makes the two mouse rows look 2 to 7 times larger
than the human rows by unit artifact alone. Split the axis or
convert to a common time unit.
4. **Fig 3 does not plot its own punchline.** The 0-of-29 rescue
result, the figure's reason to exist, appears only in the caption.
Add it to the figure (for example, a rescue annotation or a second
small panel), or the figure is a bar chart of near-identical counts
(2/13/4/29 vs 3/15/5/25) that a reader cannot distinguish.
5. **Fig S4 is a table, not a figure.** Four rows of text with
colored dots belong in a table. It also reports Harries and Peters
as "5 blood cell groups" and "16 blood cell groups" without saying
what happened in Gao heart, leaving the reader to assume both
cohorts.
6. **The Abstract omits three caveats that change the story.**
Heart Shannon is null (minus 0.100, p 0.054) while the Abstract
reports only the blood fall; TRMT11's moderated check is unverified
and SEA-AD failed, while the Abstract reports only the replication;
GTEx whole blood, the one positive transport, is not mentioned at
all (Abstract, lines 31-33). A reader of the abstract alone gets a
cleaner paper than the one that follows.
7. **Jargon audit: the no-jargon rule did not reach the body.**
P1m 39 occurrences, P0m 16, both-pass 24, E1 22, E2 23, MDE80 13,
"624-test"/"431-test" family codes 9/3. Undefined codes in the body:
M9, M10 (line 407); LACA (line 399); HCATA (lines 411, 622);
"multihot" score (lines 277, 506); cell-type codes EN_L3_5_IT_3
(lines 269, 449), IN_SST (line 279), All_Cells (lines 255, 277,
295, 299). Line 53 says "the P1 analysis neither confirms nor
refutes" where it means the present mature analysis; P1 is the
young-to-old signature, which is not what is being discussed.
A figure-only reader was protected; a text reader was not.
8. **Small figure nits.** Fig 2 panel (b) y-label reads "Center
shift, standardized (SD)" while panels (a) and (c) read "within
cell type (SD)"; the difference is meaningful (pooled vs
stratified) but unexplained in the figure. Fig S3's Gao footnote
numbers (slopes minus 0.008, p 0.77 and minus 0.062, p 0.085) are
not traceable in the main text. Fig S5's error bars on the null
bars have no definition in the figure or its caption.

## What would change the verdict

REJECT stands unless the authors do the positive-control package:
re-derive the mature signature with both sexes at old age, validate
transport on held-out mouse donors, and test a progeroid model so the
transport test has a known-positive arm. In parallel, replicate the
five fraction shifts and the narrowing slopes in an independent cohort
and run the GTEx whole-blood composition adjustment. Those are new
analyses, not reframing, which is why the verdict is reject rather
than major revision: the paper as written cannot answer the question
it poses at the standard this venue requires.

## 1. Primary-Source Verification and Metadata Reconciliation

All 7 single-cell 10x Genomics v3 matrices (`GSM6605086` to `GSM6605092`) were verified directly against `Data S21` (`41467_2024_47264_MOESM24_ESM.xlsx`) and `Data S2` (`41467_2024_47264_MOESM5_ESM.xlsx`) before count aggregation:

1. **Age reconciliation (`24y` vs prose `"26-28 years"`)**: Across all 7 GEO sample records, `Data S2` (`age` column = `2y` vs `24y`), `Data S21` (`age.months` = `24.0` vs `288.0`), and the published peer-review file (authors' response on pp. 25-26: `"~24 years old"`), the aged NMR group is `24 years` (`288 months`) vs young `2 years` (`24 months`), whereas the Methods narrative text refers to `"26-28 years old"`. Because only two discrete age cohorts exist (`2y` vs `24y`), all models strictly use the binary indicator `old_vs_young` (`1` for `24y`, `0` for `2y`) and never a continuous age slope.
2. **Sample-to-GSM alignment and 100% barcode match**: `GSM6605086` (`_2y_2_` files) maps to `Data S21` sample `nmr.2y_1` (`nmr.2y_2` barcode suffix), `GSM6605087` (`_2y_3_`) maps to `nmr.2y_2`, `GSM6605088` (`_2y_4_`) maps to `nmr.2y_3`, and `GSM6605089..GSM6605092` map 1-to-1 to `nmr.24y_1..nmr.24y_4`. Every one of the `34,923` annotated cells in `Data S2` matches cleanly to the raw integer 10x matrices (`0` missing barcodes, `0` non-integer or negative values).
3. **100% sex homogeneity and 100% breeding-colony confounding**: Per `Data S21` and the peer-review response, all `7` NMRs are **non-breeding virgin males** (`sex_male` omitted as constant). Critically, **breeding colony separates young and old with zero overlap**: all `3` young animals come from `MS 60` (`nmr.2y_1`), `MS 74` (`nmr.2y_2`), and `MS 61` (`nmr.2y_3`), whereas all `4` old animals come from `5000` (`nmr.24y_1..3`) or `25` (`nmr.24y_4`). Colony effects cannot be adjusted separately from age.
4. **FACS anti-CD3 sorting exclusion (no Family A / concordance)**: These libraries come from **anti-CD3 FACS-sorted splenocytes** (enriched to ~97-99% T/ILC lineages) rather than unbiased whole spleen. Consistent with sorted-cell rules, **Family A whole-spleen CLR cell-fraction regression and cross-cell-type concordance were NOT run** (see [`donors.csv`](../results/followup-nmr-spleen-t-gse214390-donors.csv) for descriptive sorted counts only). Notably, extreme sample-specific sorting/animal skew occurs even within the T/ILC gate: `ILC` cells make up `54.1%` (`3,448 / 6,368`) of `nmr.24y_1`, and `non.cytotoxic.gdT.cell` makes up `65.5%` (`4,589 / 7,010`) of `nmr.24y_4` (versus `1.7%` to `15.2%` across all other animals).

## 2. Explicit Preprocessing and Multiple-Testing Families

- **Gene indexing and technical masking**: Matrices were indexed by unique 10x feature IDs (`51,418` rows, Col 1 of `genes.tsv.gz`, `ENSHGLG*` and numeric NCBI RefSeq IDs) so zero duplicated gene symbols collapsed prior to normalization. `199` technical features were masked before `log2(CPM + 1)` normalization: all `38` NMR mitochondrial contig genes (`ENSHGLG00000000001..ENSHGLG00000000038`, where Ensembl symbols omit the `MT-` prefix, e.g., `ND1`, `COX1`, `ATP6`, `CYTB`), plus `RP[LS]*`, `MRP[LS]*`, `ERCC-*`, and sex-chromosome markers resolved via `genes.tsv` Col 2 and [`nmr-ensg2symbol.tsv`](../data/reference/nmr-ensg2symbol.tsv).
- **Eligibility rule**: Animal pseudobulks required `>= 20` cells in `>= 2` young and `>= 2` old animals (`residual_df >= 3`). Three primary subsets qualified (`CD4.T.cell`, `CD8.T.cell`, `non.cytotoxic.gdT.cell`), whereas `cytotoxic.gdT.cell` (`1, 0, 2` cells across the 3 young animals) and `ILC` (`0, 0, 1` cells across the 3 young animals) were excluded per [`summary.csv`](../results/followup-nmr-spleen-t-gse214390-summary.csv).
- **Multiple-testing families (stated prior to correction)**:
  - **Family B (primary donor-level gene age OLS)**: `39,792` tests across the `3` eligible primary subsets (`13,389` in `CD4.T.cell`, `13,105` in `CD8.T.cell`, `13,298` in `non.cytotoxic.gdT.cell`), model `~ old_vs_young + log_lib`, corrected globally with Benjamini-Hochberg (`q_bh_global`). Within-subset BH (`q_bh_within_group`) is reported separately as exploratory context only. Pooled pseudo-bulk sensitivities (`All_Splenic_T_Cells`, `13,446` tests; `CD3plus_T_Cells_excl_ILC`, `13,432` tests excluding `ILC` contamination) are held outside the primary `39,792`-test Family B.
  - **Family C (`P1_Clean` mouse signature composite)**: Mouse-derived `139`-gene `P1_Clean` (`89` UP, `50` DOWN) mapped via strict `ortholog_one2one` in [`orthologs-mouse-nmr.csv`](../data/reference/orthologs-mouse-nmr.csv) (`105/139`: `67` UP, `38` DOWN; primary) and via an extended exact 10x symbol sensitivity (`113/139`: `72` UP, `41` DOWN). Family C contains `family_c_size = 3` primary subsets, each evaluated against `2,000` size-matched random gene-set permutations (`empirical_null_pvalue`, BH-corrected across the `3` primary subsets as `q_bh_empirical`). Pooled sensitivity runs (`family_c_size = 1` each) are reported separately.

## 5. Interpretation and Limits (Consistent with Null Cross-Species Conservation)

1. **No contradiction of Agescape's established null transport result**: Agescape established that the mouse `139`-gene `P1_Clean` signature fails to transport positively into human aging cohorts (`q >= 0.052` in human PFC). In aged naked mole-rat splenic T cells (`n=7`), **zero positive transport is observed**; every tested subset shows a negative point estimate (`-0.260` to `-0.448`), and the single subset passing `q_bh_empirical < 0.05` (`non.cytotoxic.gdT.cell`, `n=6`) reflects an opposite-sign shift (driven partly by lower AP-1 / immediate-early transcripts `Fos`/`Jun`/`Jund` in aged colony `5000`/`25` males) that vanishes when either `nmr.24y_4` or breeding-colony structure is disturbed.
2. **Severe structural limits**: (a) `n=7` animals (`3` young, `4` old), `df = 3` to `4`; (b) `100%` male (`0` females); (c) `100%` age-colony collinearity (`MS` young vs `5000`/`25` old); (d) FACS anti-CD3 enrichment precluding unbiased cell-proportion or whole-organ claims; and (e) incomplete naked mole-rat Ensembl `1:1` orthology (`105/139`, expanding only to `113/139` via symbol lookup). Consequently, `GSE214390` cannot establish conserved organismal aging trajectories and serves only as an exploratory, directionally negative naked mole-rat sorted-T comparison.

# Mature-to-old audit

## Summary

This read-only audit covers the current manuscript at `report/plos-one/final-plos-one.tex`, all `reports/followup*.md` reports, their aggregate result tables, and direct age metadata where available. Most headline transport, mouse full-range, human adult, and Gao/Yazar follow-up results are MIXED or YOUNG-DRIVEN, not mature-to-old. The strongest existing exceptions are the 18-plus mouse composition, Bayesian drift, lung entropy, and 18-versus-30-month variability refits; the 30-plus human composition, MSSM interneuron, and MSSM depth refits; direct analyses in cohorts whose entire tested range is at least 30 years; and the old-versus-middle-aged subcontrasts that use only donors aged 40 years and older. P1 and P0 are not merely full-range signatures: their committed derivation is specifically 3 months versus 18 months. Also, the reported 18-month-reference drift estimate still regresses all 23 mice, so it is not itself a mature-only contrast. The locked 624-family result, its deferred-selection disclosure and promoted-test sensitivity failures, the primary 2,000-draw null, mouse concordance q = 0.0879, the zebrafish limitation, and the power-interpretation wording are recorded as current decisions, not reopened here. Manuscript-location verification confirms that TODO item 3 is NOT implemented: the abstract still says "Mouse profiles drifted from young references (p = 0.000004)" at tex line 37, and the Results whole-profile drift section reports the young-reference arm (+0.1027/+0.1028) at tex lines 274 to 277 before the 18-month-reference arm (+0.0072, p = 0.6656) at tex line 281. No model was rerun and no manuscript file was changed.

## Audit rules and direct age evidence

An analysis is MATURE-TO-OLD only when both derivation or selection and the tested contrast use mouse ages at least 18 months or human ages at least 30 years. Mouse old means 30 months. A continuous mouse mature fit can use 18, 21, 24, and 30 months; the requested pairwise signature derivation must be exactly 18 months on the mature side and 30 months on the old side. Human continuous mature fits use all eligible donors aged at least 30 years. Existing human bins are YOUNG = 20 to 39, MID = 40 to 59, and OLD = at least 60. Thus OLD versus MID has mature donors on both sides, but OLD versus YOUNG does not. A young-derived gene set cannot be converted into a mature-to-old signature merely by scoring older humans. NOT APPLICABLE covers non-mouse and non-human arms without a defined maturity cutoff, technical controls, metadata-only reports, and blocked reports with no age model. UNTESTABLE records a missing required input or ineligible contrast, never a null.

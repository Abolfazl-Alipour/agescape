# 11pt Follow-Up Block 1: Long-Style Per-Donor Residual Dispersion and Composition Matching (Gao Heart + Yazar Blood)

Tests whether per-gene cell-to-cell transcriptional dispersion rises with age after removing the per-donor mean-variance trend (motivated by Long et al. 2025, *Cell Reports* 44(12):116612, DOI `10.1016/j.celrep.2025.116612`), and whether any effect survives composition matching (within-state fixed-weight averaging and donor IPW reweighting). All analyses are evaluated separately for Gao Heart and Yazar Blood and never pooled across datasets.

## 2. Pre-Specified Protocol (Frozen Before Running)

- **Inputs (local, no new downloads):**
  - Gao Heart (`GSE290367_integrated_RNA_adata.h5ad`, `layers/counts`): 117 primary donors (ages 20 to 91; 20 YOUNG, 53 MID, 44 OLD, 0 LT20), 7 studies (6 study dummy indicators + intercept with `ENCODE v4 (Snyder)` reference, matching `STUDY_DUMMY_MAP`), 901,946 kept nuclei, 522,761 eligible nuclei at post-mask UMI >= 1500, 15,930 retained genes (185 technical genes excluded: 13 MT-, 95 RP[L/S], 72 MRP[L/S], 5 sex, 0 ERCC; 0 duplicates collapsed), 13 `final_cell_type`s + pooled `All_LV_Nuclei`, 62 `cell_type_plus_subcluster` states.
  - Yazar Blood (`yazar_1e44db10.h5ad`, `X`, reusing `YAZAR_CACHE` and dropping doublet rows labeled `peripheral blood mononuclear cell`): 981 donors (ages 19 to 97; 5 LT20, 103 YOUNG, 193 MID, 680 OLD), 75 pools (`pool_number` fixed effects), 1,248,849 kept cells, 617,760 eligible cells at post-mask UMI >= 1500, 35,307 retained genes (10 duplicate symbols collapsed by sum prior to masking; 211 technical genes excluded: 13 MT-, 109 RP[L/S], 84 MRP[L/S], 5 sex, 0 ERCC), 28 non-doublet `cell_type`s + pooled `All_Cells`, 30 `predicted.celltype.l2` states.
- **Human technical gene mask (Shared Rule 7):** Excludes uppercase `MT-`, `RP[L/S]` (matching repo `technical_gene_mask` regex `^RP[LS]`), `MRP[L/S]`, `XIST`, `TSIX`, `DDX3Y`, `EIF2S3Y`, `UTY`, `KDM5D` (plus `ERCC` regex, 0 present in either cohort).
- **Full-depth proportions and eligibility floors (Shared Rules 1, 4, 6, 11):** Donor is the unit in every model and permutation. Primary and composition-matching arms use full-depth post-mask raw counts converted to within-cell proportions (`p_{i,g} = c_{i,g} / U_i`); post-mask `U_i >= 1500` is an eligibility filter only (never downsampled in primary). Eligibility floors: >= 20 eligible cells per donor per cell type (or state); >= 6 eligible donors per cell type for slopes plus `_prune_covariate_matrix` rank/df gates (`min_batch_donors=2`, `min_residual_df=5` Gao / `10` Yazar for batch-adjusted arms; `min_residual_df=5` for unadjusted); >= 6 eligible donors per age bin (enforced both pre- and post-singleton-batch pruning) for group comparisons and IPW reweighting. Age bins reuse `assign_age_bin` unchanged (`LT20` < 20, `YOUNG` 20 to 39, `MID` 40 to 59, `OLD` >= 60). Units below any floor are reported `UNTESTABLE`, never as nulls and never substituted with unadjusted fallbacks. Block 1 does not use a distance-to-reference metric, so it uses a donor-permutation null rather than a split-half null.
- **Per-donor residual dispersion (frozen linear fit method):** Within each eligible `(dataset, group, donor)`, compute exact two-pass float64 mean `mu_g`, unbiased sample variance `v_g` (`ddof=1`), and detection fraction `f_g` across that donor's cells, enforcing strict non-constancy (`k_g >= 1`, `v_g > 0`, `p_max > p_min`). Fit **LAD (L1 median linear regression)** of `log(v_g)` on `log(mu_g)` (natural log) across `S_10% = {g : f_g >= 0.10, non-constant}` (requiring `|S_10%| >= 10`) using exact directional-derivative bisection returning the canonical midpoint of the optimal slope interval and symmetric median intercept `median(y - beta * x)`. Why LAD on `S_10%` instead of OLS: (1) OLS fitted on `S_10%` forces `mean(r) = 0` identically for every donor by the normal equations; (2) OLS fitted on all detected genes (`k >= 1`) creates severe cell-count horizon bias (`-log N`), a degenerate slope-2 singleton ray (`y = log N + 2x`), and a `+1.5 * Var(log U)` proportion step artifact. LAD on `S_10%` is parameter-free, scale-invariant to proportion scaling, eliminates singleton/horizon artifacts, and retains 50 percent breakdown protection for gene-level directionality. Per-gene residual `r_{d,c,g} = log(v_g) - (alpha_hat + beta_hat * log(mu_g))` is evaluated on all non-constant genes; per-donor summary `Y_{d,c} = mean_{g in S_10%}(r_{d,c,g})` measures upper-tail excess log-dispersion above that donor's constitutive median linear trend. Uniform multiplicative variance shifts (`v_g -> k * v_g`) are tracked separately via scale-invariant centered trend height `h_hat_{d,c}` and `diag_shared_trend` (Section 5).
- **Five required model arms (`results/followup-11pt-block1-residual-dispersion.csv`):**
  1. `primary`: Donor-level OLS of `Y_{d,c}` on `age_per_decade + sex_male + batch` (Gao 7 studies; Yazar 75 pool fixed effects).
  2. `within-state` (Composition Matching Arm a): Recompute `Y_{d,s}` within each fine-grained state (`cell_type_plus_subcluster` in Gao; `predicted.celltype.l2` in Yazar). Because donors vary in which rare states reach >= 20 cells, naive per-donor renormalized averages leak state intercepts under the sharp null, while complete-case drops 80 to 97 percent of Gao donors. We therefore fit primary batch-adjusted OLS within each evaluable state (`>= 6` donors, passing `_prune_covariate_matrix`, `max h < 1 - 1e-4`), require `n_states_evaluable >= 2` and `young_weight_coverage >= 0.50`, combine slopes with fixed global YOUNG-donor cell-proportion weights `w_tilde_s`, and compute exact donor-clustered influence-function HC2 variance with variance-weighted harmonic-mean residual df (algebraically identical to fixed-weight donor-average OLS on complete-case data). Cell types with only 1 state in the h5ad (26 of 28 Yazar `cell_type`s; Gao `Epicardial` and `Mast`) or only 1 evaluable state (Yazar `natural killer cell`; Gao `LEC` and `Neuronal`) are explicitly marked `NOT_APPLICABLE_SINGLE_STATE` / `NOT_APPLICABLE_SINGLE_EVALUABLE_STATE`, excluded from the `within-state` BH family, and barred from tautologically confirming or conflicting in verdicts.
  3. `reweighted` (Composition Matching Arm b): Exclude `LT20` (`n <= 5`), run `_prune_covariate_matrix` FIRST, and enforce `min(n_YOUNG, n_MID, n_OLD) >= 6` both pre- and post-pruning. Compute `+0.5` pseudocount Aitchison CLR on donor cell-state counts (`within_celltype_substate` for multi-state cell types, `tissue_all_states` for pooled, `global_donor_composition` for single-state cell types), drop zero-SD columns, project via PCA capped at `min(K-1, floor(n_min/2), 10)` inside a 5-fold stratified CV `Pipeline` selecting L2 ridge `C in {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00}` (seed `20261117`, outcome-blind), form Hajek-stabilized truncated IPW (`clip` to `[q01, q99] intersect [0.1, 10.0]` prior to bin-mean renormalization so post-Hajek max weight may slightly exceed 10), and fit WLS with Pustejovsky-Tipton IPW-HC2 and exact O(N p^2) IPW Bell-McCaffrey Satterthwaite `nu_BM(Omega = I)`, `df_eff = min(nu_BM, ESS_total - p)`. Hard `UNTESTABLE` gates: `min_bin_ESS < 6.0`, `ESS_total - p < min_residual_df`, `nu_BM < 5.0`, `min(1 - h_tilde_i) < 0.01`, `min(s_i/w_i) < 1e-4` (equivalent to `sqrt(s_i/w_i) < 0.01`, matching the linear scale of `1 - h_tilde_i` because `s_i/w_i` scales quadratically in `1 - h_tilde_i` under unequal weights), or `median_smd_post >= 0.25` (3-bin max pairwise SMD on original CLR features). Companion diagnostic: Li and Li (2019) Generalized Overlap Weights (`reweighted_overlap`, gated by the same ESS/df/leverage floors) and donor stratified pairs bootstrap (`B = 500`, dropping rank-deficient resamples).
  4. `unadjusted`: Primary outcome `Y_{d,c}`, OLS on `age_per_decade + sex_male` (`min_residual_df=5`). When `primary` is untestable on a cell type (e.g., Yazar `plasmablast`), `outcome_sd_ref` records the `unadjusted` sample's own `outcome_sd` used for standardization.
  5. `rarefied` (labeled 1500-UMI sensitivity, primary model only): Per-donor `obs_idx`-sorted multinomial downsampling to 1500 UMIs (`SeedSequence([20261117, ds_code, donor_idx])`) with immediate `.eliminate_zeros()`, followed by LAD-on-`S_10%` and primary batch-adjusted OLS.
- **Gene-level secondary (`results/followup-11pt-block1-gene-fractions.csv`):** Per `(dataset, cell_type)`, primary test is batch+sex-adjusted Freedman-Lane permutation (`B = 2000`, seed `20261117`) on the fraction of genes in age-blind complete-case `G_sec` (pooled detection >= 10 percent across eligible YOUNG+OLD cells and finite `r_{d,c,g}` in 100 percent of evaluated YOUNG+OLD donors; pigeonhole fallback only if `< 50` genes) whose adjusted residual dispersion is higher in `OLD` than `YOUNG`, enforcing `n_young >= 6` and `n_old >= 6` both pre- and post-pruning. Equal-tailed two-sided `min(1, 2 * min(p_hi, p_lo))` and one-sided `OLD > YOUNG` p-values are reported, alongside unadjusted raw donor-label permutation columns on the exact same row. **BH correction across genes within each `(dataset, cell_type)` is NOT required and NOT applied**; the permutation p-value on the fraction is the gene-level test (an explicit `q_bh_across_celltypes` column is additionally provided across testable non-pooled cell types per dataset).
- **Pre-stated BH families, exact noncentral-t MDE80, and Verdict Rule:** BH `q_bh` is applied within each `(dataset, arm)` slice across `EVALUATED` non-pooled cell types only (`UNTESTABLE*` and `NOT_APPLICABLE*` excluded with `q = NaN`; pooled rows carry singleton `m = 1, q = p`). MDE80 uses exact two-sided noncentral-t (`alpha = 0.05`, `power = 0.80`) standardized by the cell type's unweighted `primary` `outcome_sd`, with pre-stated power threshold **`mde80_sd_per_decade <= 0.50` SD/decade** (`<= 0.25` high power, matching repo convention; raw log-var/decade, 4-decade percent-variance equivalent, and residual-SD scales are co-reported). Per-cell-type `PROVES` requires positive BH-significant `primary` (`q < 0.05`) and all evaluable composition-matching arms (`>= 1` evaluable) positive; `DOES NOT PROVE` requires composition-artifact collapse, significantly negative `primary`, or null `primary` with `mde80_sd <= 0.50`, provided there is no nominally significant positive conflict under 2-arm composition disagreement (applicable to both null and significantly negative primaries); otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE`. Dataset-level `PROVES` requires `>= 25` percent of testable non-pooled cell types (`>= 4/13` Gao, `>= 4/16` Yazar; min 2) to achieve `PROVES` and exceed significantly negative cell types; `DOES NOT PROVE` requires `0` `PROVES`, `>= 75` percent `DOES NOT PROVE`, and `< 25` percent composition disagreement; otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE` (naming any `PROVES` cell types explicitly). All random seeds derive deterministically from `MASTER_SEED = 20261117`.

Non-parametrically subtracting the interpolated real-cell finite-N calibration curve accounts for **66.4 percent** (`0.000302/dec`, `p = 4.32e-06`) of the primary slope and eliminates 100 percent of pure capture-yield sensitivity (`log_tot_elig` on `Y_debiased`: `p = 0.9070`), reducing the remaining age slope to `0.000153` (`p = 0.0469`) and `0.000131` (`p = 0.0818`, null) after depth adjustment. Critically, we do not claim 100 percent pure finite-N bias: decomposing `log(N) = log(N_tot_elig) + log(ct_frac)` reveals a genuine `log(ct_frac)`-specific residual coupling (`beta = -0.00123`, `p = 3.53e-08` on `Y_debiased`; Wald test `beta(log_tot_elig) != beta(log_ct_frac)`: `t = 4.48`, `p = 8.84e-06`), consistent with Azimuth `CD4 Naive/TCM` boundary spillover or thymic-involution heterogeneity. Adding `log(N)` collapses primary `naive CD4 T` to `0.0001` (`p = 0.1802`), and `+N+Depth` yields `0.0001` (`p = 0.3399`). Crucially, `naive CD4 T` has zero internal substate resolution (`within-state` N/A), and its confirming `reweighted` arm carries three severe causal caveats: (1) it conditions on global 30-state PBMC composition (`comp_scope = global_donor_composition`), not internal substates; (2) `14/30` states remain imbalanced `SMD > 0.25` after IPW (`CD8 Naive = 1.35`, `CD4 TCM = 0.74`, `NK = 0.60`), with `medSMD = 0.23` squeaking below `0.25` only because ultra-rare zero-inflated states dilute the unweighted median; and (3) IPW **steepens** within-pool `log(N)` decline (`-0.0696` -> `-0.0961/dec`, `p = 4.80e-06`), inflating the reweighted slope from `0.0005` to `0.0007`. Controlling IPW for `+N+Depth` yields a null (`0.0002`, `p = 0.0823`), and Li and Li (2019) Overlap Weights are already non-significant (`0.0005`, `p = 0.0545`; `+log(N)`: `0.0001`, `p = 0.3700`; `+N+Depth`: `0.0001`, `p = 0.6152`). Finally, `unadjusted` (`-0.0001`, `p = 0.5503`), `rarefied 1500` (`0.0001`, `p = 0.6741`, where downsampling halves `|S_10%|`, zeroes `Var(log U)`, flattens `pcorr(Y, logN)` to near zero, and widens SE 2.4x), `Gene Frac OLD>Y` (`0.524`, `p = 0.1749`), `Shared YOUNG Trend` (`p = 0.8064`), and `Centered Height h_hat` (`p = 0.3864`) are all null.
  - *Consistent `+N+Depth` cross-check across all alternative trend estimators (eliminating finite-N double standards):* Unadjusted `Gamma-GLM` and `OLS-All` exhibit strong negative finite-N bias (Table 5.2), inducing spurious age slopes in both directions across Yazar cell types (including strongly negative unadjusted `naive CD8 T` `Gamma-GLM` `p = 1.87e-12` and `Shared YOUNG Trend` `p = 8.16e-08`, where `log(N)` drops `-0.1965/dec`). Once adjusted for `+N+Depth`, **0 of 16** non-pooled Yazar cell types reach `p < 0.05` in `Gamma-GLM +N+Depth` (`naive CD4 T p = 0.1729`, `naive CD8 T p = 0.4646`) and **0 of 16** reach `p < 0.05` in `OLS-All +N+Depth` (`naive CD4 T p = 0.7617`, `naive CD8 T p = 0.3619`). Similarly, `central memory CD4 T` `Shared +N+Depth` (`0.0004`, `p = 0.0140`) corresponds to only 0.15 percent variance over 4 decades, fails BH across the `15` evaluated non-pooled types (`q = 0.2107`), and is null in `Gamma-GLM +N+Depth` (`p = 0.8663`) and `OLS-All +N+Depth` (`p = 0.5913`). Note also that `mean_resid_shared_trend` is evaluated on donor-varying `S_10%(d)`, which introduces a small `(beta_d - beta_shared) * (mean_log_mu_s10 - ref_x)` term when `|S_10%|` shifts (explaining nominal `gamma-delta T` `Shared +N+Depth p = 0.0349`).
  - *Pooled `All_Cells` and coarse-lineage reconciliation (leave-one-state-out proof + PBMC composition collapse):* Inside `All_Cells` `within-state`, `CD4 Naive` carries a renormalized YOUNG weight `w_tilde = 21.5` percent (raw YOUNG share `20.5` percent; `CD8 Naive` adds `w_tilde = 12.4` percent) and `45.6` percent of YOUNG weight in coarse `CD4_T`. Leave-one-state-out directly proves `CD4 Naive` drives both signals: dropping ONLY `CD4 Naive` collapses `All_Cells` `within-state` from `0.0002` (`p = 0.0032`) to `0.0001` (`p = 0.1642`), and dropping `CD4 Naive + CD8 Naive` yields `0.0000` (`p = 0.7036`); likewise, dropping `CD4 Naive` collapses coarse `CD4_T` `within-L2` from `0.0002` (`p = 0.0013`) to `0.0001` (`p = 0.3937`), and adding `+N+Depth` collapses both `All_Cells` `within-state` (`0.0001`, `p = 0.2181`) and `CD4_T` `within-L2` (`0.0001`, `p = 0.1604`), matching `All_Cells` `primary` (`p = 0.4324`), `reweighted` (`p = 0.9286`), and `Overlap-Wt` (`p = 0.4676`). Similarly, the 4 nominally positive diagnostics on pooled `All_Cells` (`Shared +N+Depth p = 2.09e-05`, `Gamma-GLM p = 0.0026`, `OLS-All p = 1.29e-04`, `Gene Frac = 0.5432`, `p = 0.0140`) are driven by PBMC cell-type composition and N/depth shifts: adjusting `All_Cells` for `+N+Depth + top-15 state fractions` collapses `Shared Trend` (`p = 0.6351`) and `Gamma-GLM` (`p = 0.9010`), `+N+Depth` alone collapses `All_Cells` `Gene Frac` from `0.5432` to `0.5074`, and `OLS-All` shrinks by more than 60 percent (`0.0019` -> `0.0007`, `p = 0.0190`, reflecting residual sub-`S_10%` singleton genes).
- **Reconciliation with A2 variance and Long et al. (2025):** In Yazar Blood, A2 between-subject pseudobulk variance (`followup-between-subject-variance-yazar.csv`) showed statistically significant *negative* LVR across 8 testable lineages (`-0.163 to -0.335`) and *positive* LVR only in `naive CD4 T` (`+0.318`) and `naive CD8 T` (`+0.622`), both of which experience steep thymic cell-count decline (inflating finite-cell pseudobulk sampling variance in OLD donors). Removing the per-donor mean-variance trend in Block 1 eliminates all 8 A2 negative shifts (yielding high-power nulls `MDE80 = 0.059 to 0.146 SD/dec` across the 5 high-power memory T, NK, and CD14 monocyte lineages; plus central memory CD8-positive, alpha-beta T cell `0.290`, mucosal-associated invariant T cell `0.473`, regulatory T cell `0.505` [INCONCLUSIVE]) and shows that the lone positive LAD signal in `naive CD4 T` disappears after finite-N calibration + depth (`p = 0.0818`) or `+log(N)` (`p = 0.1802`). Finally, the Yazar 5-lineage coarse diagnostic directly reconciles Block 1 with Long et al. (2025): pooling `CD14` (`mean Y = 0.0298`) and `CD16` (`mean Y = 0.0307`) monocytes adds between-substate mixture variance (`pooled mean Y = 0.0346`) alongside monocyte `log(N)` expansion, creating an unmatched rise (`0.0004`, `p = 0.0196`) that collapses under L2 matching (`0.0001`, `p = 0.4045`); coarse `CD4_T` (`0.0003`, `p = 1.16e-04`) reflects both L2 composition mixing and `CD4 Naive`'s finite-N artifact (collapsing to `p = 0.1604` under `within-L2 + N+Depth` and `p = 0.3937` without `CD4 Naive`); and `B`, `CD8_T`, and `NK` are null on unmatched LAD (`p = 0.0527 to 0.6850`). Overall, full-depth per-donor residual dispersion provides no replicated evidence of an intrinsic age-related transcriptional noise rise in either human heart or blood once composition, ambient-RNA, and cell-count effects are accounted for.

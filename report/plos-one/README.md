## What changed in Rev2 stage 1

Headline is now the middle claim only: no uniform transcriptomic program was detected in the surveyed datasets, with upstream causes not settled and shared mechanisms with cell-type-specific consequences not excluded. The Introduction adds an operational definition of "universal" (same direction in every cell type, overlapping gene sets, comparable lifespan-scaled magnitude, present in each species tested).

- Methylation clock (H1, Universal Clock 2) deleted entirely, including its Methods subsection, Results paragraph, Discussion and Limitations mentions, and references. Reference list renumbered to 24 entries, all cited in text.
- Proliferation analysis deleted from the main text entirely.
- Off-program conclusion rewritten consistently in the abstract, Results, Discussion, and Conclusions: no statistically significant increase was detected in mouse lung at the tested depths, but confidence intervals were wide and biologically relevant increases were not excluded. Scoped to mouse lung only.
- Results updated with the verified Rev2 reanalysis numbers: joint donor-level concordance null (mouse within-tissue plus 0.187 vs null plus 0.138, p = 0.043; cross-tissue not significant); vector depth survival with dispersion dead at every fixed depth; the 18-month reference arm showing drift concentrated in the young-to-mature transition; human vector interval reported honestly with no equivalence claim; exact sign test replaced by a donor-cluster bootstrap; human lung club and multiciliated findings downgraded to between-study associations; signature method sensitivity stated (moderated WLS 151 calls vs DESeq2 312 vs unmoderated WLS 1; 310 of 765 P1 instances flip sign on leave-one-donor-out).
- Theory cut to a short predictions-only section; no verdicts on whole theories. Only the same-cell multiome test is kept as the next step.
- Meta-analysis removed from the main text; kept as a short text note in Supporting Information.
- Figure 5 (AI-generated illustration) and the schematic summary subsection removed. Supporting Figures S1, S2, and S3 removed: S1 was AI-generated, S2 lists the deleted proliferation and clock arms and gives theory verdicts, and S3 states the human vector null is evidence of absence, a framing now dropped.
- Appendix: Evidence and Caveats deleted. All process residue removed (reviewer-response language, phase labels, commit hashes, file paths, submission status). Acknowledgements dropped. A cohort table (Table 1) was added to Methods; cells that could not be verified are marked "not reported". The Bayesian Methods subsection was deleted (no Bayesian results paragraph added). Figure 1 caption now states its selection rule (25 results passing false discovery rate correction within their own strata at pseudocount 0.5, sorted by slope; 21 of the 25 also pass the wider global correction).
- Data Availability gives the repository URL (https://github.com/Abolfazl-Alipour/agescape) and notes a Zenodo archive is pending with no DOI claimed.

## What changed in Rev2 stage 2 (refresh pass)

Both stage 1 pending items are now resolved, and the "Pending refresh" section is closed.

- Signature transport claim DROPPED. The MSSM matched random signature null landed (200 expression-decile-matched random signatures per cell type, seed 20261105, age-plus-sex OLS, BH over the 21 MSSM types; `results/reviewer-rev2-transport-null.csv`): neither global-BH survivor survives it. L2/3 neurons: empirical p 0.0249, q 0.1045. Oligodendrocytes: empirical p 0.0050, q 0.0522, just above 0.05. No cell type survives both the global correction and the matched null, in either cohort (PsychAD null already failed, smallest q 0.1007, and was not re-run: PsychAD is absent from the Census release used here and its pseudobulk needs Synapse authentication). The signature Results subsection, the Discussion validation paragraph, and the Conclusions now state plainly that the mouse-derived signature did not transport to either human cohort beyond expression-matched random signatures. All "MSSM null pending" language is removed. The three global-BH survivors and their slopes/q values are still reported as the primary-correction facts, with the null verdict attached.
- Human interneuron moderated redo LANDED. New Methods subsection describes the precision-weighted models with empirical-Bayes moderated standard errors, the replication rule, and the hypergeometric and coexpression-block permutation nulls. Results now use moderated counts: MSSM FDR VIP 219, pvalb 145, sst 385, GABA 72; HBCC 213, 220, 583, 40. Both-cohort same-direction FDR: VIP 77, pvalb 65, sst 239, GABA 0 (381 total), against hypergeometric expectations 22.5, 16.5, 113.3, 1.5 and block permutation p 0.001 for the three subtypes. Genome-wide effect correlations are now the primary figures (VIP r 0.722, pvalb 0.684, sst 0.761); selected-gene correlations are secondary. Leave-one-donor-out refits are stable: no sign flip in 782 gene-by-cohort refits, median largest single-donor shift 0.48 standard errors. The "targeted" framing is softened: the same models across all 39 tested MSSM and HBCC cell types find broad age-associated expression change (MSSM microglia 814, oligodendrocyte 604, L2/3 562; HBCC L2/3 682), so the three interneuron subtypes replicate strongly as part of broader change, and an exclusive interneuron-targeted claim is not supported. The generic GABAergic neuron type does not replicate at all. PsychAD adjudication of the interneuron genes could not be run (same Census/Synapse block as above) and is stated as not done, not pending. The Gene Ontology sentence was dropped: it was computed on the baseline gene sets, was not re-run under the moderated sets, and its reference (Gene Ontology resource) is removed, renumbering the list to 23 entries, all cited in text.
- Figure 4 REBUILT from the moderated files (`results/reviewer-rev2-interneuron-genes-moderated.csv`, 768 genes, and `results/reviewer-rev2-replication-moderated.csv`), same design and filenames: MSSM vs HBCC slope scatter, all 768 moderated MSSM FDR genes as background, 381 both-cohort FDR genes ringed, generic GABAergic neuron type in grey. Side panel updated (VIP 219, pvalb 145, sst 385 genes; 730 of 768 same direction; both-cohort FDR 77/65/239).
- Abstract (247 words) and Conclusions updated: transport sentence now states the claim is dropped; interneuron sentence carries the not-exclusive qualification.
- Committed with this revision: the five Rev2 result CSVs (transport null, human DE moderated, replication moderated, interneuron genes moderated, interneuron all-cell-types) and the two watcher-patched scripts (`scripts/run_rev2_de_interneuron.py`, `scripts/run_rev2_transport_null_h5ad.py`: OOM and allow_pickle fixes that produced the final results). `results/_wtest2.txt` (2-byte scratch file) was NOT committed.
